# Two Sum

**Difficulty:** Easy

**LeetCode:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach

Use two nested loops to check every pair of elements in the array. If the sum of two elements is equal to the target, return their indices.

## Time Complexity

O(n²)

## Space Complexity

O(1)

## Notes

The solution returns the indices of the two numbers whose sum is equal to the target. The program was tested with typical and edge cases before submitting to LeetCode.
